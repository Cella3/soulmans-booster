# Soulmans Sound Booster — Music Player 3.0

An ad-free Android music player with the original Soulmans portrait art, optional sound boost and seven-band EQ, plus four sound-effect pads. Built for Android 8 and later. No account, ads, analytics, internet, or microphone permission.

## Music player

- Choose one or more audio files or a folder and its subfolders using Android Files.
- Keep a local library, saved playlists, playback history and play counts.
- Resume the saved queue and position. Playback can continue with the screen off, Bluetooth controls and a media notification.
- Seek through tracks and adjust the pitch from -12 to +12 semitones, saved per track.
- Embedded album art appears while the track plays; the new portrait is used when a file has no artwork.
- Assign one sound file to each of four pads. Trigger samples over the song, stop them individually or together, and adjust their volume.
- Choose among neon spectrum, waveform, orbital pulse or artwork-glow visuals. Fullscreen mode is available, with an optional 15-second auto-launch.

## Sound controls

Boost and the seven-band EQ apply directly to music played inside Soulmans. EQ presets include Flat, Bass, Vocal, Bright, Rock, Electronic and Acoustic, with custom bands from 60 Hz to 12 kHz. The original device-wide experimental boost remains for other apps; Android and Bluetooth routes may not support that global effect. The app does not boost phone calls.

Supported audio formats depend on Android's installed codecs. Common formats such as MP3, AAC/M4A, FLAC, WAV, Ogg and Opus are supported where the device can decode them. Some DRM-protected or unusual files may not play.

The source archive includes the buildable Android project and artwork prompt. The private signing key is excluded. If an imported file is moved or a folder grant is revoked, choose it again in Android Files.
